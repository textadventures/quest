Your game works, you have played it through, and now you want other people to play it.

How you do that is the same whatever you built, so it has a section of its own: [Publishing your game](/publishing) covers what to check before you release, getting other people to test it, and the three things the editor's **Publish** dialog can make - a `.quest` file for textadventures.co.uk, or an HTML version you can host anywhere.

One piece of advice before you go: however tempting it is, do not release the game you have built while working through this tutorial. Write something of your own first, and test it properly.

## The finished game

If you want to compare your game with ours, or you skipped ahead and would rather read the finished thing than build it, here it is: [tutorial-game.aslx](/examples/tutorial-game.aslx). Download it and open it in the editor with "Open a game file…", or with "Save to folder…" if you want to keep editing it.

It's the game as this tutorial leaves it, minus the exercises - a lounge, a kitchen, a dead man, a bee, and a locked back door worth ten points.

## What next?

That's the end of the tutorial - you now know enough to write a complete game. Here are some things you'll probably want next, as your game needs them:

- [Exits](/howto/rooms/exits) - everything on the _Exit_ tab, including exits that run a script instead of moving the player.
- [Doors, locks and keys](/howto/rooms/doors) - a door the player can open and close from both sides, and combination locks.
- [Asking the player](/howto/scripting/asking-the-player) - ask for typed input, a yes or no, or a choice from a menu.
- [Talking to characters](/howto/characters/talking) - Ask/Tell topics and [Pages](/tutorial/using-pages) for conversations.
- [Score, health and money](/howto/score/score-health-money) - achievements, and the health and money features we only mentioned in passing.
- [Handling light and dark](/howto/rooms/light-and-darkness) - rooms the player needs a light source to see in.
- [Pictures, sound and video](/howto/media/pictures) - a game doesn't have to be only text.
- [Changing what the game says](/howto/text/messages) - rewrite Quest Viva's own built-in messages in your own voice.

And when you want to see a whole small game written up from beginning to end, the next page works through [Cloak of Darkness](/tutorial/cloak-of-darkness) - the little game that everybody who writes an interactive fiction system builds first.

If you get stuck, ask on [Discord](https://textadventures.co.uk/community/discord) or in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/textadventures/quest/discussions).
