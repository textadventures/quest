Your game works, you have played it through, and now you want other people to play it.

How you do that is the same whatever you built, so it has a section of its own: [Publishing your game](/publishing) covers what to check before you release, getting other people to test it, and the three things the editor's **Publish** dialog can make - a `.quest` file for textadventures.co.uk, or an HTML version you can host anywhere.

One piece of advice before you go: however tempting it is, do not release the game you have built while working through this tutorial. Write something of your own first, and test it properly.


## What next?

That's the end of the tutorial - you now know enough to write a complete game. Here are some things you'll probably want to learn about next, as your game needs them:

- [The text processor](/howto/text/text-processor) - vary descriptions with `{if}`, `{once}` and `{random}`, and add links to objects and commands, without writing any script.
- [Exits](/howto/rooms/exits) - including locked exits, and exits that run a script.
- [Asking the player](/howto/scripting/asking-the-player) - ask for typed input, a yes or no, or a choice from a menu.
- [Talking to characters](/howto/characters/talking) - Ask/Tell topics and [Pages](/tutorial/using-pages) for conversations.
- [Score, health and money](/howto/score/score-health-money) - the built-in versions of what we built by hand with status attributes.
- [Handling light and dark](/howto/rooms/light-and-darkness) - rooms the player needs a light source to see in.
- [Debugging your game](/howto/testing/debugging) and [walkthroughs](/howto/testing/walkthroughs) - check your game still works as it grows.

If you get stuck, ask on [Discord](https://textadventures.co.uk/community/discord) or in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/textadventures/quest/discussions).
